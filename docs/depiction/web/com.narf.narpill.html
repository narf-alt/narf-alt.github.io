NarPill Status Bar Utility

NarPill — Two Pills. Endless Possibilities.

Meet NarPill, a powerful status bar utility featuring two capsules packed with customization, style, and functionality.

Battery Capsule A hollow, living battery ring that brings your status bar to life with a dynamic pulse along the battery’s circular path. Customize its colors and styles, display your own text, keep your carrier name, or show your next alarm time right inside the capsule. Explore multiple Wi-Fi icons, cellular signal styles, and many more customization options waiting to be discovered.

Clock Capsule More than just a clock! Its built-in slider lets you adjust your volume up or down quickly and effortlessly. Display the time and date, then choose from a variety of styles and colors to create a look that matches your setup.

Always With You NarPill follows you throughout your device — inside apps, in Control Center, and on the Lock Screen — keeping its unique look and useful features close at hand.

Two capsules. Countless combinations. Your status bar, your style.

Discover everything NarPill has to offer.
