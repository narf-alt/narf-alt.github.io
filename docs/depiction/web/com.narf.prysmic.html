💎 About Prysmic

Prysmic reimagines the iOS Control Center, bringing the classic Prysm-style experience back with extensive customization options for your jailbroken iPhone.

From the layout and appearance of Control Center modules to connectivity controls, sliders, media playback, and quick toggles, Prysmic lets you shape your Control Center around your preferences.

⸻

⚡ Key Features

  • 🎛️ Custom Control Center Layout: Configure the order and visibility of modules, including Connectivity, Sliders, Quick Toggles, Media, and Battery.
  • 📐 Flexible Presentation: Choose how Control Center appears, open it fully expanded, present it from the top, hide the grabber, and customize the extra row.
  • 🎨 Interface & Appearance: Customize corner styles, choose light or dark appearance options, and adjust supported interface elements.
  • 🔋 Battery & Status Indicators: Show or hide the battery widget and percentage, customize indicator placement, and choose how indicators are displayed.
  • 📶 Connectivity Controls: Customize connectivity button layouts, adjust Wi-Fi and Bluetooth behavior where supported, use Personal Hotspot in place of the AirPlay/AirDrop button, and enable haptic feedback.
  • ☀️ Brightness & Volume Sliders: Customize slider shapes, swap their positions, show percentage labels, and choose slider interaction effects.
  • 🎵 Now Playing Customization: Control media module visibility, display playback progress, use circular progress around artwork, and customize audio route visibility.
  • ⚡ Quick Toggles: Configure scrollable toggle pages with access to a selection of more than 35 toggles and app shortcuts.
  • 📱 Custom App Shortcuts: Add up to five installed apps using their bundle identifiers and make them available in supported shortcut areas.
  • ↕️ Expandable Extra Row: Reveal an additional row of selected controls and shortcuts by pulling Control Center upward.
  • 📳 Haptic Feedback: Enable supported tactile feedback for buttons and slider interactions.
  • 🔄 Respring Options: Use the available respring controls to apply changes when required.

⸻

📱 Compatibility

Designed for jailbroken iOS devices. Compatibility may depend on your iOS version, device, and jailbreak environment.
