### 💎 About Prysmic
**Prysmic** is a premium, powerful customization tweak designed to completely transform your iOS interface elements. Packed with dynamic color engine support, smooth animations, and ultra-sleek UI design.

---

### ⚡ Key Features
* 🎨 **Dynamic Color Engine:** Automatically adapts theme accents to match your wallpaper or active app.
* 🎛️ **Custom Control Modules:** Reshape and restyle toggles, sliders, and status elements.
* 🔲 **Glassmorphic & Flat Styles:** Choose between modern frosted glass or crisp flat aesthetics.
* 🚀 **Lightweight & Fast:** Optimized for smooth performance without battery drain.
* 📱 **iOS 15 to 26 Compatibility:** Full support for rootless jailbreak environments.
